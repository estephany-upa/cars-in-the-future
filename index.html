Timeline

Four decades, four reinventions

2030

The software-defined EV

Solid-state packs charge in the time it takes to buy coffee. The dashboard is one sheet of glass. Highway driving is handed to the car, and over-the-air updates make last year's model feel new.

  • 10-min charging
  • Level 3 highways
  • Glass cockpit
2040

Pods that talk to the city

Steering wheels become an option box. Cabins turn into lounges, offices and bedrooms. Every vehicle shares its intent with every other one, so intersections stop needing lights.

  • Robotaxi fleets
  • V2X mesh
  • Swivel seating
2050

Bodies that change shape

Panels made of programmable composites stretch for highway cruising and tuck in for city parking. Paint heals its own scratches and harvests sunlight. Major corridors get maglev strips, and the wheels fold away.

  • Adaptive aero
  • Solar skin
  • Maglev mode
2070

The car leaves the road

Commuter pods lift off from rooftop pads and land in your driveway. The cabin unclips from the chassis and docks to your house as a room. "Car" becomes a verb for whatever moves you.

  • eVTOL pods
  • Swappable cabins
  • Vehicle as room
